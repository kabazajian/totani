\section*{Data availability}

The \fermi-LAT photon data, spacecraft files, instrument response functions
and the 4FGL-DR4 source catalog are public at the Fermi Science Support
Center (\url{https://fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov/ssc/}); the selection used here is
stated in Sec.~\ref{sec:data}. The 128 GALPROP models of the 2012 grid are
the \fermi-LAT collaboration's public release accompanying
Ref.~\cite{Ackermann:2012pya}. The six Galp21 models were computed for this
work with GALPROP v57 by Y.L.; their configuration files and the
$|\ell|,|b|\le60^\circ$ output cubes are available from the authors on
request and will be deposited with the analysis code. The flat \fermi-bubble
mask is that of Ref.~\cite{Fermi-LAT:2014sfa} and the structured map is the
residual template of Ref.~\cite{Fermi-LAT:2017opo}, both as regridded
templates in the code release; Totani's residual construction is rebuilt by
the code from the counts. The destriped Haslam 408\,MHz map
\cite{Remazeilles:2014mba} is public at LAMBDA. The eROSITA bubble
templates start from the public eROSITA half-sky image
\cite{Predehl:2020kyq}; the reconstruction of the eastern half and the
uniform and brightness lobe templates built from it are ours and are
released with the code, with the validation of Appendix~\ref{app:erosita}.
The HESTIA particle data and AHF halo profiles used in
Sec.~\ref{sec:twodeg} belong to the authors of Ref.~\cite{Muru:2025vpz} and
are available from them on reasonable request; the derived $J$-maps for
the 216 views, the annihilation spectra from PPPC4DMID
\cite{Cirelli:2010xx}, the cell libraries at $10^\circ$, $2^\circ$ and
$1^\circ$, the simulated-sky outputs and every table in this paper are
produced by the analysis code, which will be made public at
\url{https://github.com/kabazajian/totani} with an archived release on
Zenodo upon publication.
